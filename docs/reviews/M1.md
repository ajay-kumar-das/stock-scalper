## Findings during M1 (added to doc 13)
- #25 Edge-gate threshold was unattainable, and enforcing a guessed prior in research would be circular. Fixed: 0.10R threshold; research mode measures the gate.
- #26 Engine speed (~30k bars/s). Tracked for M2.
- #27 ORB thesis exit sat inside ordinary noise; exhaustion checks were missing. Fixed on rationale, before any real-data research.

## Independent adversarial code review
A separate reviewer drove the real Engine/OMS/Portfolio with a manual broker adapter and demonstrated 7 defects the suite missed (doc 13 findings 28–34), four of them critical (lost protection after an async stop reject; untracked shares after late fills; protective actions throttled; partial-exit losses invisible to the daily limit). All are fixed and each has a regression test in `tests/test_review_regressions.py`. The reviewer judged the bar fill model conservative.
